Putting it on the iPad

  1. Open the Camera app and point it at the code.
  2. Tap the yellow link that appears at the top.
  3. In Safari, tap Share — the square with an arrow coming out of it.
  4. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Open it from the home screen from now on. It fills the whole screen, with no address bar in the way, and starts counting straight away.

Every device counts into the same tallies. Open it on as many iPads or phones as you like. A tap on one shows on all the others within a few seconds. Keep rotation lock off: sideways, all 31 items fit on one screen.
